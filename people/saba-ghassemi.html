About

Saba Ghassemi is a PhD researcher in structural engineering at the University of Southampton, working within the Infrastructure Group. Her research focuses on the structural assessment of ageing bridge infrastructure, with a particular emphasis on corrosion-induced deterioration in reinforced-concrete and metallic bridge structures.

Her PhD work combines large-scale laboratory testing, advanced corrosion characterisation, structural health monitoring techniques, and nonlinear numerical modelling. A major part of her research investigates the behaviour of corroded reinforced concrete bridge deck slabs. These studies examine how corrosion affects load-carrying capacity, deformation response, cracking behaviour, stiffness degradation, and failure-mode transition. Alongside the reinforced-concrete slab work, she has contributed to research on ageing metallic railway bridges, including field monitoring, finite-element modelling, corrosion-scenario analysis, and laboratory testing of wrought-iron and steel samples from historic bridge structures. Her research is connected to the Heavy Axle Weight / ageing infrastructure research programme and has relevance to Network Rail, bridge asset management, and the safe evaluation of existing infrastructure.
